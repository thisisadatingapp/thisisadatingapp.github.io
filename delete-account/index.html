Delete Your Account

RRHEA · rrhea-app.com

You can permanently delete your RRHEA account and its data at any time — in the app, or by request here if you no longer have the app installed.

Delete in the app

This works the same on iPhone and Android, and takes effect immediately:

  • Open RRHEA and tap the Profile tab.
  • Tap the settings gear.
  • Scroll to Account Actions and tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm. If you signed in with Apple, you'll be asked to confirm with Apple once more.

Request deletion without the app

Email support@rrhea-app.com from the email address on your RRHEA account with the subject "Delete my RRHEA account." If you signed up with Apple's private relay address, send it through that relay, or include the username shown on your profile.

We'll confirm the request comes from the account holder, then delete the account and reply to let you know. We respond to requests within 48 hours.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in
  • Your profile: name, username, age, photos, bio, location, gender, orientation, severity level and Gut Tags
  • Your matches, messages and reactions, for you and for the people you were chatting with
  • Your likes and passes, boosts, Super Like balance and purchase history
  • Your push notification tokens and notification settings

Your matches see a notice that a match deleted their account. Your name is not shown.

What may be kept

  • An anonymous transaction ID for each in-app purchase, no longer linked to you, so a purchase can't be credited twice.
  • Safety reports that were already sent to our safety team, kept only as long as needed to keep the community safe.
  • Records we're legally required to keep, for no longer than required. See our Privacy Policy.

Deletion may take a short time to clear from routine backups.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription — Apple or Google bills it, and only you can cancel it:

  • iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › RRHEA › Cancel Subscription
  • Android: Google Play Store › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › RRHEA › Cancel

Questions? Contact us at support@rrhea-app.com.
