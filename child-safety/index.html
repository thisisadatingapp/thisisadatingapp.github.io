Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

RRHEA has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everyone who uses the RRHEA app and website, and to all content and conduct on RRHEA.

RRHEA is for adults only

RRHEA is a dating and friendship app for adults. You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live, if higher) to create an account. Everyone confirms their age when they sign up, and the app does not allow an age under 18.

If we learn or have reason to believe that an account belongs to someone under 18, we remove the account and its data. We may ask for verification of age before reinstating an account.

What is prohibited

The following are strictly prohibited on RRHEA and lead to immediate, permanent removal:

  • Any content that sexualizes a minor, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind — photos, videos, drawings, AI-generated or edited images, or text
  • Grooming: contacting, befriending, or manipulating a minor for sexual purposes
  • Sextortion, or soliciting sexual content or contact from a minor
  • Trafficking or the commercial sexual exploitation of children
  • Sharing, requesting, or linking to any of the above, or encouraging or normalizing the sexual abuse of children
  • Using RRHEA to arrange to meet a minor, or creating an account on behalf of a minor

How to report

If you see anything on RRHEA that may involve the abuse or exploitation of a child, please report it right away:

  • In the app: open your chat with the person, tap the ⋮ menu at the top right, and choose Report. Reports go straight to our safety team, along with recent messages from the conversation so it can be reviewed. The person you report is not told who reported them. You can also Block them from the same menu.
  • By email: support@rrhea-app.com with the subject "Child safety report". Include the username involved and what you saw. Do not attach or forward any abusive image.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (911 in the US and Canada) first. In the US you can also report directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678. In Canada, report to Cybertip.ca.

How we prevent and respond

  • Photo screening: every profile photo is checked on the device for nudity before it can be uploaded.
  • Review: every report is reviewed by our team. Reports involving a possible child safety concern are treated as the highest priority.
  • Removal: content and accounts that violate these standards are removed, and the people responsible are permanently banned.
  • Reporting to authorities: when we become aware of apparent child sexual abuse material, we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as required by U.S. law (18 U.S.C. § 2258A), and to other relevant authorities where required. We preserve the relevant information as the law requires.
  • Law enforcement: we cooperate with law enforcement investigations of child sexual abuse and exploitation, consistent with our Privacy Policy and applicable law.

Compliance with the law

RRHEA complies with applicable child safety laws in the countries where it is available, including the reporting obligations of U.S. federal law and Canadian law.
